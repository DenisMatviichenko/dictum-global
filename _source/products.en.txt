#korporatyvni-treninhy|Corporate Security Training
P We prepare your team for inspections and searches so that every employee knows their rights, roles and the rules for communicating with law enforcement.
## Training objectives
- prepare the team for inspections and searches
- explain employees' rights and obligations during visits by authorities
- prevent leaks of confidential and commercial information and information on existing violations at the company
- set rules for communicating with law enforcement
- review and improve the company's security system
- reduce the risk of pressure on employees by law enforcement
## What we do during the training
- audit the workforce
- allocate employee roles in communication with public authorities
- work through various scenarios for conduct during visits by authorities
- identify potential sources of information leaks
- define levels of responsibility and the list of employee actions
## What you get after the training
- a video recording for later review and for training other employees
- employee test results describing their behavioural patterns
- allocation of roles and responsible persons
